# Chrome Web Store copy | English

## Name

Video Speaking Coach

## Short description

Follow a simple speaking routine: watch YouTube, note keywords, retell three times, get feedback, and save your practice.

## Full description

Want to practise English speaking but unsure what to do each day? You understood the video, but can you explain it in English? Video Speaking Coach turns input, keyword recall, active retelling, feedback, and another attempt into a routine you can follow in your browser.

Watch the video, write a few keywords, then retell it three times:

1. **Say it** — look only at the keywords you wrote, then connect them aloud in your own English.
2. **Make it clear** — organize the topic, main points, and an example.
3. **Make it natural** — retell it independently and add your view.

The side panel keeps your original wording and feedback so you can see how your explanation develops. Speak with your browser's speech recognition and edit the transcript before submitting, or type your response. Basic word translation works without an API key. For contextual explanations, speaking feedback, and questions, configure your own OpenAI or Alibaba Cloud Model Studio API key. Provider charges may apply. Feedback is written guidance; this version does not provide numeric or pronunciation scores.

After three rounds, restart while keeping the previous attempt, or export the session as Markdown for import into Notion or use in Obsidian. Direct Notion sync is not available in this version.

The video stays on YouTube. The extension does not download the video or automatically read its full transcript. AI feedback uses the title, keywords, and retellings you provide.
